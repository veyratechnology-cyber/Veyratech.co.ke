### Step 3: Verify Your Business

**Google will ask you to verify. Choose one method:**

#### Option A: Postcard (Most Common)
- Google sends a postcard to your business address
- Takes 5-14 days to arrive
- Enter the 5-digit code from the postcard
- **You won't appear on Google until verified!**

#### Option B: Phone Verification (if available)
- Get an automated call or text
- Enter the code immediately
- **Faster but not always available**

#### Option C: Email Verification (rare)
- If Google has your email on file
- Click the verification link

#### Option D: Instant Verification (if you have Search Console)
- If your website is already verified in Google Search Console
- You can verify instantly

**✓ Request postcard now, don't wait!**

### Step 6: Get Your First Reviews

**Your Google Review Link:**

After verification, Google will give you a short link like:
```
https://g.page/r/YOUR_UNIQUE_CODE/review
```

**Find this link:**
1. Go to your Business Profile
2. Click "Home"
3. Look for "Get more reviews"
4. Copy the short link

**Share this link with:**
- Recent clients via email
- After successful project completion
- In your email signature
- On your website

**Review Request Email Template:**
```
Subject: Quick favor? Share your VeyraTech experience
```

### Step 7: Set Up Messaging (Optional but Recommended)

Enable messaging so customers can message you directly from Google:

1. **In your Business Profile, go to "Messages"**
2. **Turn on messaging**
3. **Download the Google Business Profile app** on your phone
4. **Respond to messages within 24 hours** (Google tracks response time)

❌ Not responding to reviews
❌ Not posting regularly
❌ Wrong business category
❌ Incorrect contact information
